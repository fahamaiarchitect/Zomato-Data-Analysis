## 📌 Project Overview

This project performs an Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on Zomato restaurant data to uncover customer preferences, restaurant trends, ratings, voting patterns, and spending behavior.

Using Python-based data analysis libraries, the project transforms raw restaurant data into actionable business insights through visualizations and statistical exploration.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The primary goal of this project is to answer key business questions:

- Which restaurant types are most popular?
- How do ratings vary across restaurant categories?
- What is the spending behavior of customers?
- How do online and offline ordering affect ratings?
- Which restaurant categories receive the highest customer engagement?

## 📊 Exploratory Data Analysis

### 1️⃣ Restaurant Type Distribution

Analyzed the distribution of restaurant categories to identify the most common business models.

**Insight:**
- Dining restaurants dominate the dataset.

---

### 2️⃣ Restaurant Type vs Votes

Examined customer engagement across restaurant categories.

**Insight:**
- Dining restaurants receive significantly higher votes than other categories.

---

### 3️⃣ Rating Distribution

Visualized customer rating patterns.

**Insight:**
- Most restaurants maintain ratings between 3.5 and 4.0.

---

### 4️⃣ Cost Analysis

Studied customer spending behavior.

**Insight:**
- Most couples prefer restaurants with an approximate spending of ₹300.

---

### 5️⃣ Online Orders vs Ratings

Compared ratings between online and offline ordering restaurants.

**Insight:**
- Restaurants offering online ordering generally achieve better customer ratings.

---

### 6️⃣ Heatmap Analysis

Performed correlation analysis between restaurant categories and online ordering.

**Insight:**
- Ordering preferences vary considerably across restaurant types.

---

## 📈 Key Findings

✅ Dining restaurants are the most popular category.

✅ Most restaurants maintain positive ratings.

✅ Customer spending behavior is concentrated around mid-range restaurants.

✅ Online ordering positively impacts restaurant performance.

✅ Customer votes strongly correlate with restaurant popularity.
